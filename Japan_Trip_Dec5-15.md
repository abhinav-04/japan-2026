### **Day 2 · Sun Dec 6** — Tokyo Culture Day
*Pace: Full but flexible. Jet lag wakes you at 5am — use it.*

- **7:30 AM** — **Sumo morning practice** 🥋 (Ryogoku) — sit ringside, watch 160kg wrestlers train in silence. No tournament = more access. **Must book ahead.**
- **10:00 AM** — **Asakusa** — Senso-ji temple (quick), then Nakamise street food
- **11:30 AM** — **Kappabashi Kitchen Street** 🔪 — where Tokyo's chefs buy their gear. Japanese knives, ceramics, plastic food replicas. Best souvenirs in Japan.
- **2:00 PM** — **teamLab Planets** ✨ — walk barefoot through water into infinity mirrors. Wear pants you can roll up. **Timed ticket.**
- **6:00 PM** — **Shinjuku nightlife** — start at **Omoide Yokocho** (yakitori alleys), then **Golden Gai** (200 tiny bars, most seat 6 people). Bar-hop, don't settle. Some have ¥500-1500 covers.
- **1:00 AM** — **Ichiran Ramen** 🍜 — the solo booth experience. Order it *kotteri* (rich) with extra garlic, firm noodles. Press the button for noodle refill.

**Get to bed by 2 AM.** Disney starts in 5 hours.

> ⚠️ **Tout warning:** Never follow someone who approaches you offering to take you to a bar (Kabukicho/Roppongi). Bottakuri scams are real — ¥100k bills, won't let you leave. Only go to bars YOU picked.

*Pace: PEAK. 25,000 steps. Worth every one.*

**Download the Tokyo Disney app BEFORE you fly.** Everything runs through it.

### **Day 6 · Thu Dec 10** — Kyoto Day
*Pace: Balanced. One early start, then chill.*

- **8:00 AM** — **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** 🎋 — empty at 8am, mobbed by 9:30. Just a corridor of 20m-tall bamboo, but it hits different.
- **9:00 AM** — Optional: **Iwatayama Monkey Park** — 20 min uphill to a clearing full of wild Japanese macaques + panoramic Kyoto views
- **11:30 AM** — **Nishiki Market** — 5 blocks of food stalls. This is lunch. Wagyu skewers, fresh yuba, soy milk donuts, sake by the cup.
- **2:30 PM** — **Sake in Fushimi** 🍶 — Kyoto's brewing district. Hit the Gekkeikan museum (ends in tasting) + brewery tap rooms along the canal.
- **4:30 PM** — **Gion at dusk** — walk Hanamikoji as lanterns come on and geiko/maiko head to appointments. Then the Shirakawa canal (prettier, quieter).
- **Night** — Kiyamachi bars or standing sake bars (*tachinomi*)

> Optional splurge: **Maiko dinner** (~¥20k pp) — private meal with performance. Book through hotel.

### **Day 7 · Fri Dec 11** — Nara → Osaka
*Pace: Balanced. Deer in the morning, neon at night.*

- **8:30 AM** — Train to **Nara** (45 min)
- **Morning** — **Nara Park** 🦌 — 1,200 free-roaming deer that bow for crackers. Buy the *shika senbei* and brace for headbutts. Funniest 90 min of the trip.
- **See:** Todai-ji (world's largest bronze Buddha), then **Kasuga Taisha** 🏮 — don't skip this: 3,000 stone lanterns lining a forest path, 10 min walk, most tourists miss it
- **Watch:** Nakatanidou near the station pounds mochi at insane speed every ~30 min
- **3:30 PM** — Train to **Osaka**, check in
- **Night** — **Dotonbori** 🌃 — the neon canal, Glico running man, mechanical crab. Eat: takoyaki, okonomiyaki, kushikatsu. Then dip into **Ura-Namba** (back alleys behind Namba station) where locals actually go.

*Pace: PEAK. Sleep in first, then go all in.*

- **Sleep in.** You've earned it.

- **Midday** — **Osaka Castle** + park

**Before bed:** Use takkyubin (luggage forwarding) to send big bags to your Dec 14 Tokyo hotel (~¥2k). Pack light for Hakone — lots of transport switches.

  - **Lake Ashi pirate ship** + Hakone Shrine torii in the water

  - Change into yukata (robe)

> **Onsen etiquette:**
> - Wash completely at seated showers BEFORE entering bath
> - No swimsuits (everyone's naked, stops being weird in 90 seconds)
> - Small towel never goes in water — fold it on your head
> - **Tattoos banned at many onsen** — check policy before booking or reserve a **private bath (*kashikiri*)**

## 🍜 The Ramen Plan

**Ichiran** (the solo booth chain) is *good* but engineered for eating alone. Four of you in four separate boxes is weird for a group trip.

**Better plan:** Hit Ichiran at **1am after Golden Gai (Dec 6)** when the booth makes sense. Order *kotteri* (rich) with extra garlic, firm noodles.

**Better bowls for other slots:**
- **Tokyo Ramen Street** (Dec 7, Tokyo Station basement) — 8 famous shops, pick your own. Try **Rokurinsha** for tsukemen (dipping noodles).
- **Kinryu** (Dec 12 late, Dotonbori) — 24hr, standing counter, giant dragon over the door
- **Fuunji** (Shinjuku) — Tokyo's most famous tsukemen. Worth the queue.
- **Afuri** (Harajuku/Shibuya) — yuzu shio (citrus salt broth), light and clean when you're sick of heavy food

**Etiquette:** Slurp loud (aerates broth, it's expected) · eat fast, don't linger · buy ticket from machine before sitting · water is self-serve · no tipping · best shops are cash-only

## 💡 Things Nobody Tells You

- **No tipping.** Don't. It's confusing at best, rude at worst.
- ***Otoshi*** at izakaya = small compulsory appetizer/seat charge (~¥300-500 pp). Not a scam, just how it works.
- ***Nomihodai*** = all-you-can-drink for 90-120 min, flat fee. Great value for four people.
- **Last trains = ~12:30am.** Miss it and a taxi across Tokyo is ¥5k-10k.
- **Escalators:** Stand LEFT in Tokyo, RIGHT in Osaka. Locals notice.
- **7-Eleven ATMs** take foreign cards. Carry ¥10k-15k cash — best bars are cash-only.
- **Coin lockers** at every major station (¥400-700). Use them on checkout days.
- **Sunset = 4:30pm in December.** Already baked into every day above.
