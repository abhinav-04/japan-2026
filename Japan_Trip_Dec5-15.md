## 💡 Things Nobody Tells You

- No tipping, ever.
- *Otoshi* (izakaya seat charge, ~¥300 to 500 pp) is normal, not a scam. Street touts in Kabukicho/Roppongi are: only go to bars you picked.
- *Nomihodai* = all-you-can-drink flat fee, great value for four.
- Last trains ~00:30. Escalators: stand LEFT in Tokyo, RIGHT in Osaka.
- 7-Eleven ATMs take foreign cards. Carry ¥10k to 15k; best bars are cash-only.
- Sunset is ~4:30 PM in December.
