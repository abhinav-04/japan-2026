# 🇯🇵 Japan Group Trip — Dec 4 to Dec 15, 2026

**Squad:** 4 travelers · **Style:** Mid-range (business hotels / good hostels, private rooms for 4) · **Season:** Early December (cool, ~5–13°C, autumn leaves lingering + Christmas illuminations)

> **Route:** Tokyo → Hakone → Kyoto → Nara → Osaka → back to Tokyo (Disney finale)
> Arrive **Narita**, fly out of **Haneda** on Dec 15. The trip loops back to Tokyo Bay, so you end right next to your departure airport — no cross-country rush on the last day.

---

## ✈️ Quick logistics (read first)

- **JR Pass:** Buy/activate the **7-day Japan Rail Pass** around Day 5 to cover the long Shinkansen legs (Tokyo ↔ Kyoto ↔ Osaka ↔ Tokyo). Compare against individual tickets before buying — the pass got pricier in recent years.
- **IC cards:** Everyone grab a **Suica or ICOCA** (or mobile version) on arrival for metros, buses, convenience stores.
- **Pocket WiFi / eSIM:** One eSIM each (Ubigi/Airalo) or rent **one pocket WiFi** for the group.
- **Cash:** Japan still loves cash. Withdraw yen at **7-Eleven ATMs**. Carry ¥10,000–15,000 on you.
- **Luggage:** Use **takkyubin** (luggage forwarding) between cities so you're not dragging bags on trains.
- **Booking priority:** Lock the **Hakone night**, the **Disney-area / Haneda nights (Dec 13 & 14)**, and **dated Disney tickets** first — all sell out fast.

**Personal flights:**
- **Vansh:** Arrives **Narita (NRT) Fri Dec 4→5**, 4:30 PM (WestJet YYZ–NRT via Calgary). Departs **Haneda (HND) Tue Dec 15, 10:10 AM** (Cathay Pacific HND–BLR via Hong Kong).

## 📅 Day-by-Day

### Day 1 — Fri Dec 4 · Depart Toronto
WestJet YYZ→NRT, 9:35 AM departure, 1 stop in Calgary (~1h35m). ~17 hrs total; you land the next day. Sleep on the plane, hydrate.

---

### Day 2 — Sat Dec 5 · Arrive Tokyo
- Land **Narita 4:30 PM**. Clear customs, grab Suica cards + pocket WiFi.
- **Narita Express / Skyliner** into the city; check in.
- Easy first evening (jet lag): dinner + walk around **Shibuya** or **Shinjuku**.

---

### Day 3 — Sun Dec 6 · Tokyo (East Side)
- **Asakusa** — Senso-ji Temple + Nakamise shopping street.
- **Akihabara** — arcades, anime, electronics, retro gaming (great group fun).
- **Shibuya Crossing** at night + izakaya dinner.

---

### Day 4 — Mon Dec 7 · Tokyo (West Side)
- **Meiji Shrine** → **Harajuku / Takeshita Street** (quirky fashion, crepes).
- **Shinjuku** afternoon: Tokyo Metropolitan Gov't Observatory (free Fuji views on a clear day).
- Evening: **Omoide Yokocho** yakitori alley or **Golden Gai** tiny bars.

---

### Day 5 — Tue Dec 8 · Hakone (Mt. Fuji + Volcano + Onsen)
- **Odakyu Romancecar** from Shinjuku (~90 min). Forward big luggage ahead to Kyoto.
- **Hakone Open-Air Museum** → **Ropeway over Owakudani** (active volcanic valley — sulfur vents + black eggs) → **Lake Ashi** pirate ship with Mt. Fuji views.
- **Night in a ryokan / onsen stay** — the one splurge. Kaiseki dinner + hot spring baths.

---

### Day 6 — Wed Dec 9 · Hakone → Kyoto
- Shinkansen from Odawara to **Kyoto** (~2 hrs). Drop bags.
- **Fushimi Inari Shrine** — thousands of red torii gates (go later afternoon).
- Evening: **Gion** geisha district + dinner on **Pontocho Alley**.

---

### Day 7 — Thu Dec 10 · Kyoto
- **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** early + Tenryu-ji garden.
- **Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)**.
- **Nishiki Market** food crawl.
- Night: early-December **temple illuminations** (Kodai-ji / Eikan-do) if dates line up.

---

### Day 8 — Fri Dec 11 · Nara day trip → Osaka
- Morning **Nara** (45 min from Kyoto): bowing **deer** + **Todai-ji** giant Buddha.
- Afternoon: train to **Osaka**, check in.
- Evening: **Dotonbori** — neon canal + street food (takoyaki, okonomiyaki, kushikatsu).

---

### Day 9 — Sat Dec 12 · Osaka
- **Osaka Castle** + park.
- **Kuromon Ichiba Market** (seafood, wagyu, snacks).
- **Shinsekai** retro district for kushikatsu. Optional: Umeda Sky Building at night.

---

### Day 10 — Sun Dec 13 · Osaka → back to Tokyo
- Morning Shinkansen Osaka → **Tokyo** (~2.5 hrs). Use JR Pass.
- Afternoon: last-minute **shopping** (Ginza / Shibuya / Nakano Broadway), souvenirs.
- **Sleep in the Tokyo Bay / Maihama area** (near Disney) to set up tomorrow.

---

### Day 11 — Mon Dec 14 · Tokyo Disney 🏰 (finale)
- Full day at **Tokyo DisneySea** *(recommended for the group — unique to Japan, great theming, the new Fantasy Springs area, and it serves alcohol)* **or Tokyo Disneyland** (the classic).
- **Weekday = shorter lines**, and it's peak **Christmas season** — decorations + evening illuminations.
- Buy **dated tickets in advance online** (they sell out); use the **Tokyo Disney Resort app** for Standby Pass / Priority Pass and show times.
- **Sleep near Haneda tonight** (see lodging) for the early flight.

---

### Day 12 — Tue Dec 15 · Departure
- Be at **Haneda by ~8:00 AM** for the **10:10 AM** Cathay flight (HND → Hong Kong → Bangalore).
- From a Haneda-area hotel it's a short hop; from the Disney/Bay area allow ~60 min. 🎌

## 🏨 Where to stay (group of 4)

Pick **private 4-bed rooms** or two twins in the same place.

**Tokyo (Days 2–4, 3 nights)**
- **Nui. Hostel & Bar (Kuramae)** — famous social hostel, great bar, private rooms.
- **UNPLAN Shinjuku / Kabukicho** — central, clean, sociable.
- **Sotetsu Fresa Inn / Tokyu Stay (Shinjuku or Shibuya)** — solid business hotels.

**Hakone (Day 5, 1 night) — the splurge**
- **Hakone Tent** — social hostel *with its own onsen* + bar (best value group pick).
- Or any **Hakone-Yumoto / Gora ryokan** with in-room kaiseki (~¥15,000–25,000/person).

**Kyoto (Days 6–7, 2 nights)**
- **Piece Hostel Kyoto** — by Kyoto Station, great design, private rooms.
- **Len Kyoto Kawaramachi** — hostel + café-bar, central to nightlife.

**Osaka (Days 8–9, 2 nights)**
- **The Blend Inn** or **Osaka Ukiyo-e Hostel** — group rooms near Namba/Dotonbori.
- **Hotel Hillarys Shinsaibashi** — affordable business hotel in the heart of it.

**Disney night (Day 10, Dec 13) — Tokyo Bay / Maihama**
- **Tokyo Bay area hotels** (Hilton Tokyo Bay, Sunroute Plaza, or cheaper Shin-Urayasu business hotels) for easy park access next morning.

**Last night (Day 11, Dec 14) — near Haneda**
- **Villa Fontaine Premier / Grand Haneda Airport** (connected to the terminal — zero stress), or any Ota-ku / Keikyu-line business hotel with quick Haneda access.
