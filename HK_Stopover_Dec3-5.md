### **Day 2 · Wed Dec 4** — 🏰 Hong Kong Disneyland
*Pace: Full park day. The newest "World of Frozen" land is here.*

- **07:30** — Leave hotel
- **08:30** — At the gate (park opens 10:00, but arrive early for rope drop)
- **Buy dated tickets online ahead** — cheaper + skips the ticket queue (HK$639-699 depending on date)
- **All day:**
  - **World of Frozen** (opened 2023) — Arendelle village, Frozen Ever After boat ride, Wandering Oaken's Sliding Sleighs
  - **Mystic Manor** — Hong Kong exclusive, trackless dark ride (one of the best Disney attractions anywhere)
  - **Big Grizzly Mountain Runaway Mine Cars** — HK-exclusive coaster
  - **Iron Man Experience** — Marvel 3D ride
  - Castle show, parade, and **evening fireworks**
- **9 PM** — Leave after fireworks
- **Dinner** — Quick bite in the park or back in the city (late-night **wonton noodles** or **roast goose**)

**Hong Kong Disneyland is smaller than Tokyo Disney** — one full day covers it well. It's the warmup before DisneySea.

**Download the Hong Kong Disneyland app** before you go for wait times and mobile food ordering.

## 🏨 Where to Stay in Hong Kong

**You need 2 nights (Dec 3 & 4).** Two rooms, same as Japan.

### Recommended Areas:

**Tsim Sha Tsui (Kowloon side):**
- Close to MTR, waterfront, night markets
- Airport Express to Kowloon Station (20 min)
- Hotels: **Hotel Ease Tsim Sha Tsui**, **The Salisbury YMCA** (harbor views, budget pick), **Kerry Hotel** (splurge)

**Hong Kong Island (Central/Admiralty):**
- Airport Express to Hong Kong Station (24 min)
- More expensive, closer to business district
- Hotels: **mini hotel Central**, **The Upper House** (luxury)

**Near Disneyland:**
- **Hong Kong Disneyland Hotel** or **Disney Explorers Lodge** — stay in the park, walk to the gate. Convenient but limits your HK exploration time.

**Recommendation: Stay in Tsim Sha Tsui.** Best balance of location, cost, and local flavor.

You only have 1.5 days, so hit the classics:

|------|-------|-----|
| **Dim sum** | Tim Ho Wan, One Dim Sum | Michelin-starred, cheap, iconic |

Street food at **Temple Street Night Market**: curry fish balls, stinky tofu, egg waffles.

## 🚇 Getting Around Hong Kong

- **Octopus Card** — Hong Kong's Suica. Buy at the airport, tap on/off MTR, buses, ferries, even convenience stores.
- **Airport Express** — HK$115 one-way (25 min to Kowloon, 24 min to Hong Kong Station). Pre-book online for discounts.
- **MTR (subway)** — Fast, cheap, everywhere. Disneyland has its own MTR station (Disneyland Resort Line).
- **Taxis** — Red taxis serve all HK Island/Kowloon, cheap (flag drop ~HK$27).

## 💡 Hong Kong Tips

- **Language:** English is widely spoken. Cantonese is the local language, Mandarin works too.
- **Tipping:** 10% at sit-down restaurants (sometimes already added). No tipping at cha chaan tengs or street stalls.
- **Currency:** HK Dollar (HKD). 1 HKD ≈ ₹11 or CA$0.17. ATMs everywhere.
- **Weather in early December:** Mild (15-22°C), bring a light jacket.
- **No VPN needed** (unlike mainland China).
